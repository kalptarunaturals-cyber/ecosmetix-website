Skin care manufacturing

Skin care manufacturer for serums, creams and more

From Grade 1 daily care to Grade 4 advanced correction: acne, pigmentation, anti-ageing, under-eye and barrier repair, built on proven actives and delivery systems.

Skin care built on active science

Our skin care formulas pair proven actives (niacinamide, azelaic acid, salicylic acid, tranexamic derivatives, retinol, peptides, bakuchiol) with delivery systems that reduce irritation and improve performance.

  • Serums, creams, gels, lotions, cleansers and toners
  • Pimple patches and bi-phasic serums
  • Under-eye creams including retinol, exosome and peptide variants
  • Anti-pigmentation: an advanced alternative approach to hydroquinone
  • Barrier repair with ceramides, Aquaxyl™ and Pentavitin
  • Dermatologically aligned formulas for sensitive skin

FAQ

Skin care manufacturing: questions

Which skin care products can you make?

Serums, creams, gels, lotions, foam and gel cleansers, exfoliating toners, under-eye creams, pimple patches, bi-phasic serums, lip care and sunscreens.

Do you have ready formulas?

Yes. Our 2026–27 range includes graded formulas for acne, oily skin, under-eye, pigmentation and anti-ageing.

Can you support claims?

We help plan claims and testing (dermatological testing, user trials) so that every claim on your pack is backed by data.
