Body Lotion Manufacturer in India: Types, Formulation, Packs, Rules and Questions to Ask

22 November 2026 · By the Ecosmetix R&D team

Body lotion is a volume product. A customer uses a palmful on arms and legs every day, so a 200 ml bottle empties in a few weeks and the next order arrives quickly if the texture and fragrance felt right. That repeat cycle is why picking a body lotion manufacturer matters more than the low unit price of the category suggests. A lotion that pills under clothing, smells different from batch to batch or thins out in a hot warehouse will cost you reorders long before it costs you reviews.

Below we walk Indian brand founders through what a body lotion project actually involves: the product types that sell, how the emulsion is built, the choice between private label and a custom formula, packaging, the licence and label rules, a realistic sequence of stages, and the questions worth asking any factory before you sign. We develop and fill body care for brands at our plant in Ambala, Haryana, so where it helps we also explain our own way of working.

What a body lotion manufacturer does for your brand

A third party body lotion manufacturer holds the cosmetic manufacturing licence, the equipment and the quality system. Your brand owns the name, positioning, artwork and sales. In between sits a set of tasks that can be split in different ways depending on how much you want to build yourself.

  • Formula work: adapting a ready base or developing a new one around your actives, skin feel and cost target.
  • Samples and approval: lab samples, rounds of feedback and a locked formula with a written specification.
  • Testing: stability in the final pack, preservative efficacy, and any claim tests you plan to print.
  • Production: raw material checks, batch manufacturing, in-process and finished product QC, filling, coding, cartoning and dispatch.
  • Your side: target buyer and price, brand story, artwork approval, marketplace listings and marketing.

If you are new to the routes on offer, our guide to private label, contract manufacturing and custom formulation lays out the trade-offs in detail.

How a body lotion is formulated and made

Almost every body lotion is an emulsion: tiny droplets of oil held evenly in water by an emulsifier. Michigan State University's Center for Research on Ingredient Safety groups typical lotion ingredients into emollients, emulsifiers, fragrances, humectants, occlusives, pH adjusters, preservatives and solvents. Their balance decides how the lotion feels on the first stroke and an hour later.

On the plant floor the sequence usually runs like this. The water phase and the oil phase are weighed and heated separately so that butters and waxes melt fully. The oil phase is then brought into the water phase under high shear in a homogeniser, which breaks the oil into fine droplets; droplet size has a large effect on how stable and how smooth the lotion is. The batch is cooled with slow stirring, and the ingredients that dislike heat, such as many actives, fragrance and parts of the preservative system, go in during this cool-down stage. pH is checked and adjusted, then the batch is sampled for release tests before filling.

What decides the skin feel

  • Emollient choice: light esters spread fast and vanish; heavy oils and butters linger and feel richer.
  • Emulsifier and thickener: they set the viscosity, the pump-ability and whether the lotion feels watery or cushiony.
  • Humectant level: enough gives a fresh, dewy feel; too much can leave skin tacky, especially in humid weather.
  • Silicone or silicone-free: silicones add a powdery slip; a silicone-free brief needs other emollients to avoid drag.

Actives and honest claims

Urea is a good example of why the dose and the claim must go together. A 2013 review of the clinical literature in Dermatology Online Journal describes urea as hydrating at lower levels and as having a keratolytic, smoothing action at higher levels. So a lotion with a little urea can be sold for hydration, while a smoothing claim needs a formula, and ideally data, that justifies it. The same thinking applies to lactic acid, niacinamide and kojic acid. Cosmetics in India may not make drug claims, so words such as "cures" or "treats" stay off the pack whatever the active level.

Delivery systems are another lever. Our R&D team works with liposomal, microsponge, encapsulation and Z-Pods systems, which can help an active stay stable in an emulsion or release more gradually on skin. Our explainer on liposomes, microsponges and encapsulation covers when they are worth the added cost.

Packaging choices for body lotion

The pack changes the product. It decides the viscosity range the formula must hit, how air and fingers reach the lotion and what the unit cost looks like. Settle the pack before the final sample round, not after, because a lotion tuned for a jar may be too thick to pump.

  • Pump bottle: the usual choice for 200 ml to 400 ml lotions. Hygienic and easy to dose, but the lotion must be fluid enough to prime the pump and the pump has to be tested for clogging.
  • Flip-top or squeeze tube: good for travel sizes, hand creams and smoothing treatments. Tubes protect actives from air better than open-neck bottles.
  • Jar: suits body butters and very rich creams. Exposure to fingers and air is higher, so the preservative system and active stability need extra attention.

Ask for a stability test in the exact final pack, including the pump or cap, since some packaging materials can interact with fragrance or oils. Our plant fills pump bottles, tubes and jars across common sizes.

Licence and labelling rules for body lotion in India

Body lotion is a cosmetic, so it falls under the Drugs and Cosmetics Act and the Cosmetics Rules, 2020 published by CDSCO. It must be made at a licensed site. A brand without its own factory usually works either through a manufacturer's own licence or through a loan licence on the manufacturer's premises; our cosmetic loan licence guide explains the difference.

Two sets of label rules apply at the same time. The Cosmetics Rules cover items such as the product name, the ingredient list, the batch number, the manufacturing licence number, the manufacturer's name and address and a use-before date. The Legal Metrology (Packaged Commodities) Rules, 2011 add consumer declarations such as net quantity, maximum retail price, the month and year of manufacture or packing and consumer care contact details. On quality standards, the Bureau of Indian Standards publishes IS 6608 for skin creams, and a capable manufacturer should be able to tell you which of its tests they run on a cream or lotion. Check the latest amendments with your regulatory adviser before printing, as these rules are updated from time to time.

Questions to ask a body lotion manufacturer

Put these to every plant on your shortlist and ask for the answers in writing.

  1. Do you hold a valid cosmetic manufacturing licence for this site, and will the product be made under your licence or a loan licence?
  2. Is the formula a private label base or new work, and who owns it once the project is done?
  3. Which stability tests will you run, for how long and in which pack, including the actual pump or cap?
  4. How do you test that the preservative system works for this formula and this pack?
  5. What evidence supports each claim on my label, and which claims would you advise me to drop?
  6. What is the minimum order per SKU and per pack size, and what drives it: batch size, packaging supplier minimums or printing?
  7. Which release tests do you run on each batch, and will I receive a certificate of analysis?
  8. Who checks my label content against the Cosmetics Rules and Legal Metrology rules before printing?
  9. What capacity is free if my order volume doubles in six months?

Common mistakes in body lotion launches

  • Briefing a "rich" lotion for a pump pack. The texture you loved in a jar sample may refuse to pump. Fix the pack early.
  • Testing only in winter. A lotion that feels perfect in a cool Delhi December can feel sticky in a humid Mumbai monsoon. Try samples in more than one season or climate if you sell nationally.
  • Claims ahead of data. "48-hour hydration" or "visibly smoother in 7 days" needs test results behind it. Without them, describe the experience instead.

Frequently asked questions

How do I start my own body lotion brand in India?

Decide the body concern and buyer first, then pick a route: a private label base for speed or a custom formula for a unique product. Work with a licensed manufacturer, approve samples, run stability in the final pack, check your label against the Cosmetics Rules and Legal Metrology rules, and launch with one or two SKUs.

Do I need a licence to sell a body lotion under my own brand?

The lotion must be manufactured at a licensed site under the Cosmetics Rules, 2020. A brand without a factory usually sells product made under the manufacturer's licence or under a loan licence held on the manufacturer's premises. Confirm the right arrangement for your case with a regulatory adviser.

What is the minimum order quantity for a body lotion?

It depends on the product, the pack size and how much is customised. Batch size, packaging supplier minimums and label printing all play a part. Share your SKU sizes and launch volume and the manufacturer should confirm the MOQ per SKU in writing.

How long does it take to manufacture a private label body lotion?

Timing depends on sample rounds, testing and packaging lead times. At Ecosmetix first lab samples typically take about 14 days; stability checks, artwork and packaging sourcing follow before the production batch.

What makes a body lotion non-sticky?

Mostly the balance of emollients, humectants and thickeners. Light esters absorb quickly, while too much humectant or a heavy butter load can feel tacky, especially in humid weather. A good formulator tunes this balance to your climate and pack.

Which ingredients are used in body lotions for rough, bumpy skin?

Smoothing lotions commonly use gentle exfoliating acids such as lactic acid together with urea in a moisturising base. Our range includes lotions with 10% lactic acid and urea. The claim on the pack should match what the formula and its data support.

Can a manufacturer make a matching body wash and body lotion?

Yes. Many brands launch a wash and lotion pair built around one concern, such as hydration or even tone, so the products share a fragrance and story. Brief both together so the formulas and packs are planned as a set.

Should I choose a pump bottle, tube or jar for body lotion?

Pump bottles suit fluid daily lotions, tubes suit travel sizes and treatment lotions and protect actives from air, and jars suit thick butters. Fix the pack before the final sample round and test stability in that exact pack.
