Teen skin care manufacturing for your brand

Teen skin care is about building a first routine that works without irritating young skin. Ecosmetix develops gentle face washes, oil-control moisturisers, clarifying serums, pimple patches and lightweight sunscreens for teen-focused brands. Our graded system lets you pick milder Grade 1 and 2 formulas for daily care, with encapsulated actives such as salicylic acid for lower irritation.

Which grades suit teen skin?

Grade 1 and Grade 2 formulas are usually the starting point: gentle actives for daily use, with Grade 3 options for persistent concerns.

Can you build a complete teen routine?

Yes: cleanser, serum, moisturiser, sunscreen and pimple patches developed as one matched line.

Do you use encapsulated actives?

Yes. Encapsulated and controlled-release actives help keep formulas gentler on young skin.

How fast can I launch?

A lab sample is typically ready within 14 days of an approved brief; private label formulas can be faster.
