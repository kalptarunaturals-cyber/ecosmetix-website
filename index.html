Questions

Frequently asked

Quick answers for brand founders. More on our FAQ page.

What is the minimum order quantity (MOQ)?

MOQ depends on the product type and pack. Share your brief with SKU size and launch volume and we will confirm MOQ and costing for your exact product.

How fast can you develop a new formula?

Most formulations reach the lab-sample stage within 14 days of an approved brief, and 99% of our formulas are approved at the first selection.

Do you work with startups and new D2C brands?

Yes. Many of our clients are new-age D2C brands. We support you from concept, actives and claims to packaging and manufacturing.

Which products do you manufacture?

Skin care (serums, creams, cleansers, sunscreens, patches), hair care (shampoos, conditioners, masks, serums), body care, baby care, lip care and pregnancy care.

Where is Ecosmetix located?

Our R&D centre and manufacturing facility are at Plot No. 171, Sector 2, HSIIDC Saha, Ambala, Haryana, and we serve brands across India.
