# Ecosmetix blog plan (SEO)

Goal: rank first on Google India for third-party / private-label skin care and hair care manufacturing searches,
and turn readers into product briefs (/product-brief/) and WhatsApp enquiries.

## How each post is chosen
1. Never repeat a topic or primary keyword already on /blog/ (check blog/index.html and tools/specs/).
2. Rotate the clusters below so service pages get supporting posts evenly.
3. Prefer keywords with clear buying intent ("manufacturer", "private label", "cost", "MOQ", "how to start").
4. Check what currently ranks on Google for the keyword (WebSearch) and write something more complete, more
   specific and more useful than the top results: real process steps, tables, costs as ranges with sources, checklists.

## Clusters (pillar page -> supporting topics)
- Third-party manufacturing (/third-party-cosmetic-manufacturer-india/): third party manufacturing vs own plant;
  contract manufacturing agreement checklist; MOQ explained; how long manufacturing takes; quality control and stability
  testing; questions to ask a manufacturer; factory audit checklist.
- Private label (/private-label-cosmetics-manufacturer/): private label skin care in India; launching with ready formulas;
  white label vs private label; packaging and labelling; choosing a first range of 3-5 SKUs.
- Custom formulation (/custom-cosmetic-formulation/): writing a brief; formulation costs; actives and percentages;
  claims substantiation; sample rounds; delivery systems (liposomes, encapsulation).
- Skin care (/skin-care-manufacturer/): serum, face wash, moisturiser, toner, under-eye, acne, pigmentation, vitamin C,
  niacinamide, retinoid, peptide products: how they are made and what to ask for.
- Hair care (/hair-care-manufacturer/): shampoo, conditioner, hair oil, hair serum, anti-dandruff, hair growth serum.
- Sun care (/sunscreen-manufacturer/): mineral vs chemical, SPF and PA testing in India, white cast, tinted sunscreens.
- Regulation and business: cosmetic manufacturing licence in India (CDSCO / state FDA), labelling rules (Legal Metrology,
  BIS), import vs local manufacture, cost to launch a brand, D2C launch checklist, pricing and margins.
- Regional (/cosmetic-manufacturer-north-india/): cosmetic manufacturer near Delhi NCR, Punjab, Haryana, Chandigarh,
  Himachal, Uttarakhand, Rajasthan; why manufacture in Ambala.

## On-page checklist (enforced by tools/blog_tool.py check)
- 1,500-2,600 words; primary keyword in SEO title (first), H1, slug, first 100 words, 2+ H2s and the meta description.
- 2+ charts/diagrams/tables made by us (inline SVG or HTML tables). No copied images.
- 6-8 FAQs written from real "People also ask" style questions (FAQPage schema is added automatically).
- 3-6 credible sources (government, standards bodies, peer-reviewed papers, industry bodies).
- 3+ internal links: the cluster's pillar page, /product-brief/, and 1-2 related posts.
- Never name competitors (BO International, Maxnova, Dermat India) or brands that must not appear
  (Cureskin, Curehair, Nature4Nature, Leafon). Clients that may be named: Numour, Just Herbs.
- Never invent numbers, studies, results or testimonials.
